The true story

Badge #17

Sirius was an explosives detection dog for the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey Police Department, posted at the World Trade Center with his partner, Officer David Lim. Together they checked suspicious packages and answered bomb threats. When the collar went on, Sirius knew it was time to work. When it came off, he went home and played with Lim's kids.

On the morning of September 11, 2001, the two were in the police office below the South Tower when the first plane hit the North Tower. Lim put Sirius in his kennel, told him he'd be back for him, and went to help people get out.

The South Tower fell with Sirius inside. When the North Tower came down, Lim was trapped in a stairwell for hours before rescuers reached him. By the time he got out, the tower where Sirius waited was gone.

Sirius was four years old, and the only police dog killed in the attacks. In January 2002, recovery workers found him in the wreckage and carried him out of Ground Zero under an American flag, with the same police honor guard given to fallen officers.

The lore

The Legend of the Dog Star

I

The brightest star

Long before there were cities, people looked up and found one star brighter than all the rest. The Egyptians watched for its return to know the Nile would flood. The Greeks set it in the jaw of the Great Dog and called it Seirios, the scorching one. For thousands of years it has been the Dog Star: the light travelers looked for so they wouldn't get lost.

II

Badge #17

In 1997 a yellow Labrador was born who would carry that name. He grew into a bomb dog for the Port Authority Police. With the collar on, he swept packages and walked the halls of the World Trade Center. With it off, he was just a good dog at home.

III

The promise

On the morning of September 11, 2001, his partner put him in his kennel, promised to come back, and ran toward the people who needed help. The towers fell. His partner survived. The tower where Sirius waited did not.

IV

Coming home

In the cold of January they found him, and carried him out under the flag the way they carried out fallen officers. That spring, more than a hundred K-9 teams crossed the river to say goodbye.

V

The watch the legend

They say a dog named after the brightest star never really leaves. He just goes back to where his name came from.

His partner promised to come back for him. So Sirius made a promise of his own: to watch over every working dog who puts on a collar and goes to work, and every one who comes home grey-muzzled when the work is done.

VI
